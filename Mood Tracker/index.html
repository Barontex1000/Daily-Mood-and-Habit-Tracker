Welcome to Your Personal Mood and Habit Tracker!

This app helps you track your daily mood and up to 5 habits. Here's how to use it:

  1. Select a date using the date picker below.
  2. Rate your mood for the selected date by clicking on an emoji.
  3. Select your gender to see relevant habits.
  4. Check off the habits you've completed for that day.
  5. Add new custom habits (up to 5) using the "Add Custom Habit" button.
  6. Click "Save Entry" to record your data.
  7. View your 7-day overview in the chart at the bottom.
